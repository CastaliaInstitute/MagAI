A certificate for people who want to be accountable for AI.

MagAI is earned through demonstrated work: study the foundations, build artifacts in a focused track, document your decisions, and defend a capstone before the credential is awarded.

Choose a track →Read an open program book

What you do

Read a graduate-style online curriculum. Build models, notebooks, workflows, and documented decisions. Practice evaluation, grounding, governance, and scoped tools. Complete and defend a substantial capstone artifact.

What you receive

A proprietary Castalia Institute MagAI certificate, a body of work you can show and inspect, a registry record when awarded, and a foundation for continued Magisterium study.

How it is taught

Public course books, structured projects, faculty critique, and a final defense. The work is online and designed for serious professionals and independent builders.

Not a badge. A body of evidence.

Your credential should say what you can do. MagAI makes the work visible: the assumptions you made, the systems you built, the tests you ran, and the judgment you exercised.

The Castalia Institute Magisterium confers proprietary credentials based on demonstrated work and evaluation. These credentials are not accredited academic degrees and do not confer professional licensure.
